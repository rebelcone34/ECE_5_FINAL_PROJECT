CONTROL SYSTEM

PID Control

How Our PID Controller Worked

PID or Proportional Intergal and Derivative control PID plays a curical role in how our robot reacts to missng the line. To keep it simple PID is essntially methods of using error to correct our robots path. Proportional reacts to our current error away from the line. Intergal accumlates the error over time to smoothly get back to line. Derivative reacts to the rate of change of the error

P

Kp

0.65

We found that having high proportional at high speeds can lead to your robot going off the track very quickly. So through trial and error we found 0.65 to be the good inbetween.

I

Ki

0.045

We found that having integral made our turns alot smoother however shifting too high resutled in unintended affects. This value provided the right about of smooth turns along with reacting fast enough.

D

Kd

0.40

Derivitive allowed us to react fast to error espically if it changes rapidly at the cost of our sensors shaking. For us we found increasing it to it current value allowed us to travl tight cornors alot easier.
